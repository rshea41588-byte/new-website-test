After a QuickBooks cleanup, what should change in your first month?

A finished QuickBooks cleanup should make next month easier. If the transactions got corrected but nobody agreed who reviews the bank feed, reconciles the statements and reads the reports going forward, the file can drift right back into the same mess.

Shea Business Solutions offers catch-up bookkeeping and monthly bookkeeping in Orlando. A review on its site describes a gym owner whose financials had fallen four years behind before Ryan reconstructed the QuickBooks file. Another client says a rebuilt file finally made the profit-and-loss report understandable. Those are two clients describing their own files. Yours might need something different.

Agree on the date the clean file begins

Ask the bookkeeper which periods got reviewed and which are still open, since a prior-year cleanup, a current-year catch-up and ongoing monthly work are different scopes. You want the handoff date in writing, plus a note on any earlier balances still under investigation. Keep that scope handy so an old question doesn't get mistaken for a new monthly error.

List each bank and credit-card account that belongs in the business books. Intuit's QuickBooks reconciliation guidance says the process compares QuickBooks transactions with the account statement. Give the bookkeeper complete statements for the period rather than relying only on whatever a bank feed happened to download.

Make the monthly division of work explicit

Decide who sends receipts and who works through the uncategorized transactions. Somebody also has to flag a payment that might be personal or a duplicate. The owner knows what a charge was for, and the bookkeeper decides how to record it from there. When each assumes the other has it, an item can sit unresolved for months.

At the first monthly reviewAsk to see
Account reconciliationWhich statements and ending dates were matched
Open questionsA short list of transactions needing your explanation
Profit and lossThe reporting period and any categories that changed during cleanup
Balance sheetUnusual balances that still need supporting records

Shea says its monthly service includes transaction categorization, bank and credit-card reconciliation, and profit-and-loss and balance-sheet reporting. Ask how they'll arrive and when you're expected to look at them, ideally while the month is fresh enough to ask why a category changed.

Keep a clear route for new questions

Nobody expects you to learn every QuickBooks setting. What matters is telling the bookkeeper early, before the next close, when you open a new bank account or add a payment processor, and any time a transaction shows up twice.

If your Orlando business has finished a cleanup or is about to start one, ask Shea to define the handoff in the written scope. The firm's monthly bookkeeping page explains its recurring service, and its contact page is the place to request a consultation.
