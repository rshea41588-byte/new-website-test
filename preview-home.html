Client Success Stories

Real-world results

How we help Orlando and Central Florida businesses clean up their accounting, stay compliant, and grow.

Orlando Construction Contractor

Challenge: 2 years behind on reconciliations, mixed personal and business expenses, facing a Florida Department of Revenue payroll tax audit.

Action: Full QuickBooks reconstructive cleanup, separated owner draws, correctly classified subcontractors, prepared tax-ready financials.

Outcome: Passed the payroll audit with zero penalties and uncovered $14,500 in missed write-offs.

Lake Nona Boutique Fitness Gym

Challenge: Messy books and inaccurate P&L reports blocked a bank loan to expand the gym space.

Action: Rebuilt the Chart of Accounts, integrated Mindbody with QuickBooks Online, implemented real-time monthly reporting.

Outcome: Verified financials secured a $75,000 expansion loan and optimized their S-Corp tax structure.

Orlando Custom Manufacturer

Challenge: A metal fabrication plant had inaccurate COGS tracking and messy raw materials inventory, distorting P&L reports.

Action: Restructured inventory tracking, linked job-costing software, set up accurate allocations for materials and direct labor.

Outcome: Precise margin reporting per production run and a corrected $22,000 inventory valuation error.

Orlando Local Restaurant

Challenge: Complex weekly payroll, tips allocation errors, and 6 months behind on bookkeeping.

Action: Corrected tip allocation, reconstructed historical bookkeeping, configured automated payroll tracking in QuickBooks Online.

Outcome: Reconciled all accounts, established payroll for 15 employees, and delivered accurate food-cost margins.

About Ryan Shea

Clean books. No surprises.

Shea Business Solutions Bookkeeping & Tax is a small business accounting firm serving the Orlando, FL area. As a QuickBooks Level 2 ProAdvisor and Certified QuickBooks Payroll specialist, I work directly with small business owners to get their finances organized, accurate, and working for them.

With over 10 years of hands-on QuickBooks and accounting experience, I've helped businesses across Orlando and Central Florida — from construction companies and restaurants to law offices and medical practices — build clean books and understand their numbers.

As Orlando's most responsive QuickBooks bookkeeping service, I am committed to a same-business-day response standard. I believe that small businesses deserve real-time support, not weeks of waiting. Whether you're starting fresh with QuickBooks, need a major cleanup, or want reliable monthly bookkeeping and tax support, I'm here to provide honest, proactive, and expert guidance.
