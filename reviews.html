The results behind the reviews

Reviews tell you how it felt to work together. These four engagements, drawn from our case files, show what the work produced.

Orlando commercial painting contractor

The business was two years behind on reconciliations, had personal and business expenses mixed together, and was facing a state payroll audit. We reconstructed 24 months of bookkeeping, separated the personal draws, and corrected the payroll tax classifications. The audit closed with zero penalties, and the cleanup surfaced $14,500 in missed tax deductions.

Lake Nona boutique fitness studio

Inaccurate financial statements were blocking a bank loan for a gym expansion. We rebuilt the chart of accounts, integrated their Mindbody booking software with QuickBooks Online, and set up monthly P&L and Balance Sheet reporting. The verified financials secured a $75,000 business expansion loan.

Orlando custom manufacturer

A metal fabrication plant had messy raw-materials inventory and distorted cost of goods sold in QuickBooks Online. We restructured the inventory tracking and the allocations for materials and direct labor, which corrected a $22,000 inventory valuation error and gave management real margin reporting per production run.

Orlando local restaurant

Complex weekly payroll, tip allocation errors, and six months of missing bookkeeping. We corrected the tip allocation procedures, reconstructed the historical records, and automated the weekly payroll configuration. The restaurant now runs reliable weekly payroll for 15 employees with accurate food-cost margins.
