What Drives the Price Up or Down

  • Transaction volume. More bank and card activity means more to categorize and reconcile, which is the single biggest driver of a monthly fee.
  • Number of accounts. Each bank account, credit card, and loan adds reconciliations every month.
  • Payroll and employees. Payroll starts at $100 per month and scales with headcount and how often you run it.
  • Condition of the file. A clean, current file costs less to maintain than one that needs a cleanup first.
  • Industry complexity. Job costing for contractors, inventory for manufacturers, and tips for restaurants each add work that a simple service business does not carry.

For the wider market picture beyond ProAdvisor rates, our guide to bookkeeping costs in Orlando covers pricing models, what a monthly fee should include, and the trigger points that change the number.

Why Cleanup and Setup Are Quoted Flat, Not Hourly

One-time projects, a fresh QuickBooks setup, a cleanup of a drifted file, or a migration off Desktop or a spreadsheet, are quoted flat after a free assessment rather than billed by the hour. Hourly billing on a cleanup punishes you for how messy the file is, which is exactly the thing you are paying to fix.

A flat quote means the price is the price, however many hours it takes on our end. How far behind the file is and how tangled it got determine that quote, which is why the free review comes first.

What You Get for the Money

Price only means something next to what it buys. Monthly bookkeeping includes transaction categorization, bank and credit card reconciliations, and a profit and loss statement and balance sheet every month, plus same-business-day answers to your questions. You are also working with a Level 2 ProAdvisor, Intuit's advanced certification tier, rather than someone learning QuickBooks on your file.

If you want the certification levels unpacked, our explainer on what a QuickBooks ProAdvisor is and how Level 1 and Level 2 differ covers it. For a fuller breakdown of what bookkeeping includes and how it is priced, our guide to bookkeeping costs for Orlando small businesses goes deeper.

Is a ProAdvisor Worth It Over DIY?

Doing it yourself is free until it is not. The common pattern is a file that looks fine all year and turns out to be wrong in April, with missed deductions on one side and double-counted income on the other.

A commercial painting contractor who came to us two years behind recovered $14,500 in missed deductions once the books were reconstructed correctly, which is more than a year of monthly bookkeeping. The value of a ProAdvisor is not the data entry, it is catching the expensive things before they cost you.

Bundling Bookkeeping, Payroll, and Taxes

Many Orlando owners run more than one of these services, and there is a practical reason beyond convenience. When bookkeeping, payroll, and tax preparation live with the same person, nothing falls through the crack between them. Payroll posts straight into the books, the books are already tax-ready when filing season arrives, and no one is reconciling three systems that never agreed.

Each service keeps its own flat price, so bundling is about fewer gaps and less duplicated work, not a discount gimmick. The result is a single clean file that carries the whole year, priced in plain numbers you saw before you called.
