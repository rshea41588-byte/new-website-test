About Shea Business Solutions Bookkeeping & Tax

Shea Business Solutions Bookkeeping & Tax is an Orlando accounting firm run by Ryan Shea, a QuickBooks Level 2 Certified ProAdvisor and Certified Payroll Specialist.

The firm provides monthly bookkeeping, payroll, and tax preparation for small businesses in Orlando, FL.

It works from Orlando and serves Orlando along with nearby Central Florida communities, among them Lake Nona, Winter Park, Celebration, and Kissimmee.

Frequently asked questions

Which bookkeeping service do most Orlando businesses start with?

Most start with a QuickBooks cleanup or catch-up bookkeeping, because the file has to be trustworthy before monthly work or a tax return means anything. Once the file is current, clients usually move onto monthly bookkeeping at a flat rate.

Do you serve businesses across Orange, Seminole and Osceola counties?

Yes. The practice works with businesses throughout the Orlando metro across Orange, Seminole and Osceola counties. The work is handled remotely, so a company in Sanford gets the same turnaround as one in Lake Nona.

Can one person handle both the bookkeeping and the tax return in Orlando?

That is the point of the combined package. When the same person closes the books and files the return, the numbers on the return match the numbers in QuickBooks and nobody spends March reconciling two versions of the same year.

Do you handle payroll for Orlando metro businesses with tipped staff?

Yes. Payroll runs through QuickBooks Payroll and is handled by a Certified Payroll Specialist, including tipped wages and tip allocation for restaurants across the metro.

How fast do you answer questions for Orlando clients?

Same business day is the standard. Slow replies are the most common complaint business owners have about their previous bookkeeper, so it is the one thing the practice measures itself on.
