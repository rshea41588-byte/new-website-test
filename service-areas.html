How the Service Area Is Drawn

The practice sits in south Orlando near the Central Florida GreeneWay (SR-417). SR-417 runs the eastern side of the metro from Sanford and Oviedo in Seminole County down through Lake Nona and into Osceola County toward Kissimmee and St. Cloud. I-4 carries the middle, from Celebration up through Winter Park. SR-429 and Florida's Turnpike handle the western side toward Apopka, Windermere and Dr. Phillips. Those three corridors are the service area. Because the work is remote, distance changes the drive and not the turnaround.

Looking for a specific service instead of a town? The full Orlando services list covers bookkeeping, QuickBooks, payroll and tax preparation. The head-term page is bookkeeping services in Orlando.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Orlando-area towns does Shea Business Solutions serve?

Twelve towns have a dedicated bookkeeping page: Apopka, Baldwin Park, Celebration, Dr. Phillips, Kissimmee, Lake Nona, Oviedo, Sanford, St. Cloud, Windermere, Winter Park and Winter Springs. Five of those also have a tax preparation page. Businesses elsewhere in the metro are welcome too.

Do you work with businesses in Seminole County as well as Orange County?

Yes. Oviedo and Sanford are both in Seminole County and both have their own page. Orange, Seminole and Osceola counties are all inside the normal service area.

Do I need to be near Orlando for you to handle my books?

No. The bookkeeping, payroll and tax work runs remotely through QuickBooks, so the drive is optional. The town pages exist because local businesses search that way, not because the service changes by town.

Can we meet in person somewhere in the Orlando metro?

Yes, when it helps. The practice is in south Orlando off SR-417, between the Beachline and Osceola Parkway. Most clients find a call or a screen share gets to the same answer faster.
