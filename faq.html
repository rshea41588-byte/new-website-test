Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from Orlando small business owners about QuickBooks, bookkeeping, and working with Shea Business Solutions.

What bookkeeping services do you offer for small businesses in Orlando?

We provide comprehensive bookkeeping services for Orlando-area small businesses, including QuickBooks setup and management, monthly reconciliations, P&L and balance sheet reporting, and tax preparation. Our goal is to give you clean books and clear financial visibility every month.

What does a QuickBooks Level 2 ProAdvisor do for an Orlando business?

A QuickBooks Level 2 ProAdvisor is a certified expert with advanced QuickBooks knowledge beyond the basic level. We can set up your QuickBooks from scratch, clean up years of messy data, build custom reporting, integrate payroll, and train your team. It means you are working with someone who knows the software inside and out.

How can I tell if my Orlando business is overpaying in taxes?

If your books are not accurate and up to date, you are likely missing deductions and overpaying. We review your financial records, identify overlooked deductions, and organize everything so nothing gets missed at tax time. Many clients are surprised how much they were leaving on the table.

Do you help with QuickBooks cleanup for messy Orlando books?

Absolutely. This is one of our most common services. Whether your QuickBooks has not been touched in years or was set up incorrectly, we do a full cleanup and rebuild so your data is accurate, your reports make sense, and you can move forward with confidence. We handle one-year and multi-year cleanups.

What types of Orlando businesses do you work with?

We work with a wide range of Orlando-area small businesses including construction companies, gyms and fitness studios, restaurants, law offices, dental practices, manufacturing businesses, bakeries, and med spas. Every industry has its own financial rhythm and we understand the differences.
